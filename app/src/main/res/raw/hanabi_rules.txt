## Objective
- **Hanabi** is a cooperative card game where players work together to create five color-coded stacks of cards in ascending order (1 to 5).
- The twist: **you cannot see your own cards**—only your teammates' cards are visible to you.

## Card Deck
- The deck contains cards in **5 colors**: Red, Green, Blue, Yellow, and White (sometimes also Rainbow in variants).
- Each color has cards numbered **1 to 5**.
- The quantity of each number in a color:
  - **Three 1s**
  - **Two 2s, 3s, and 4s**
  - **One 5**

## Setup
- Shuffle all cards and deal:
  - **4 cards per player** (for 3–5 players)
  - **5 cards per player** (for 2 players)
- Place in the center:
  - **8 clue tokens** (blue)
  - **3 fuse tokens** (red) for mistakes
  - A **discard pile**
  - Space for **5 color stacks**

## Gameplay Overview
Players take turns in **clockwise order**. On your turn, you must perform one of three actions:

### 1. Give a Clue
- Spend **1 clue token** to tell another player something about their hand.
- You may only indicate **cards of one color** _or_ **cards of one number**.
- You must point out **all** cards matching the clue.
- You **cannot** say both color and number at once or suggest what to do with the card.

**Example**: “These two cards are red.” (You point to them.)

### 2. Play a Card
- Attempt to play one of your cards onto the corresponding color stack.
- You **cannot see your own card** but must choose based on clues or intuition.

**If successful**:
- Add the card to the stack.
- If a **5** is played, **regain 1 clue token** (unless already full).

**If invalid**:
- Discard the card.
- **Lose 1 fuse token**.

### 3. Discard a Card
- Discard one card from your hand.
- **Regain 1 clue token** (unless already full).
- The card is placed in the **discard pile** (visible to all).
- Draw a new card afterward.

## Important Rules
- You may **not** communicate about your own hand outside of given clues.
- You may **not** give hints through **tone, gestures, or timing**—only through clues.
- Players must **immediately draw** a new card after discarding or playing (if cards remain).

## Winning and Losing

The game ends immediately when:
- **All 3 fuse tokens are used** → You lose.
- **The last card is drawn** → Each player gets **one final turn**.
- **All five color stacks reach 5** → Perfect game! You win!

## Scoring (optional for casual play)
- If the game ends before completion, your score is the **sum of the top cards** in each color stack.
- **Maximum score: 25**.
- Use this score to measure your teamwork and communication efficiency.

## Strategy Tips
- Give **meaningful clues** that help teammates make informed decisions.
- Manage **clue tokens** carefully.
- Discard only when you’re reasonably sure a card isn’t important.
- Track which cards have been **played** or **discarded** to improve coordination.

---

**Have fun playing Hanabi – and remember: it’s all about trust, memory, and clever communication!**
