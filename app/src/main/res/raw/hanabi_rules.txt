Hanabi – Rules

• Each player holds their cards facing outwards. You can see everyone’s cards except your own.
• The goal is to create stacks of cards in each color, in order from 1 to 5.
• You take turns giving clues, playing cards, or discarding them.
• Clues cost clue tokens. You can only give information about color or number.
• Wrong plays cost a life. Lose 3 lives and the game ends.
• Complete all stacks to win the game.

Have fun playing Hanabi!
