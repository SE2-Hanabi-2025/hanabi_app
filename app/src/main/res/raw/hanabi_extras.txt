## Bonus Tiles (Optional Rule)
You can add an extra layer of excitement and variability to the game by including **Bonus Tiles**.
Here's how to use them:

### Setup
- Take the **6 Bonus Tiles** and shuffle them **face-down**.
- Place them **in the center of the table**, forming a stack.

### How to Use Bonus Tiles
- Whenever you **complete a firework stack** (i.e., play a **5** on top of a color pile), **do not regain a clue token** as in the base game.
- Instead, **draw one Bonus Tile** from the stack.
- **Immediately apply its effect**, then **discard the tile**.
- Afterwards, **draw a new hand card** as usual to refill your hand.

### Possible Bonus Tile Effects
Here are some example effects your Bonus Tiles might include (depending on your version or house rules):

1. **Regain 1 clue token.**
2. **Regain 2 clue tokens.**
3. **Discard any one card from your hand, then draw two new cards.**
4. **Look at your own hand (once).**
5. **Give a second clue this turn without using another clue token.**
6. **Prevent the next fuse loss.**
